# Conventions for the Follow-Up Analyses, Fixed Before Anyone Looks

7 October 2026, second draft after a round of objections. One sentence or paragraph of convention per analysis, as the author asked, consolidated from four independent proposals (research log). Nothing here moves a signed line. The signed lines are in `freeze/part1.md`, line 113: the token-level split's one half, the exploited components' 90 percent, and the pin test's trigger. Everything else below is description with no line. This file is committed as `results/s6_followups/CONVENTIONS.md` before any analysis runs, and its commit hash is recorded in the research log.

**Terms.** A **setting** is one value per component, 38,912 rank-one entries and 24 delta entries. A search's **start** $s^0$ is its random starting setting. A setting's **added harm** on a batch is its harm minus the start's harm on that batch. A **cell** is one attack batch, one held-out batch, and one start. The **strata** are the shuffle's: within each weight matrix, ten usage bands of the alive components (mean label above $10^{-6}$ on the first post's 1,024 texts) and the never-needed components as an eleventh; the 24 delta entries are a twelfth group where a convention needs one. A label is **zero** when its float32 value is exactly 0.0; the label is the pre-activation clamped to [0, 1], so this is its literal output wherever the pre-activation is at or below zero (`vpd_audit_2/labels.py`, line 148). **Pooled** means a ratio of means over cells, as the carry-over ratio is pooled (research log, Appendix A).

## 1. The Starts Test

Fixed in the research log (Appendix G) and in plan 1 of the research log. Two descriptions are added at our suggestion, deciding nothing: each search's breakout step, the first step at which its two-step mean harm reaches 5 nats; and each start's 24 delta-entry values.

## 2. The Token-Level Split (signed line: one half)

> **Cells.** Every carried setting (80 attack searches at 20, 40, 80, 160, and 320 steps) on every held-out batch: the 1,280 cells per length of the step table. The own-batch version runs on the 80 attack cells per length, as description.
>
> **The two parts.** Part Z applies the setting's value $s_c$ at every position where component $c$'s label on the scored batch is zero, and the search's own start $s^0_c$ at every other position. Part P applies $s_c$ where the label is above zero and $s^0_c$ elsewhere. The 24 delta entries have no label, and a delta entry's mask is the raw setting value, exactly the mask of a label-zero component; so they take the setting's value in Part Z and the start's value in Part P. Section 8(a) measures their share separately. The labels are the ones the run's scoring used for that batch, recomputed the same way. As a check, each batch's count per position of positive labels below $10^{-3}$ is printed, so a reader sees how little a tolerance would have moved.
>
> **The share.** A part's share is pooled: its mean added harm over cells divided by the full carried setting's mean added harm over the same cells. The median over cells of the per-cell share is printed beside it.
>
> **The interval.** The signed 95 percent interval, applied to the share as it is applied to the carry-over ratio: attack and held-out batches resampled independently, the start term from the analysis of variance, 10,000 replicates (`vpd_audit_2/lead_intervals.py`). A part is **above half** or **below half** when its interval lies wholly on one side of one half, and **undetermined** otherwise.
>
> **The reading.** Part Z above half and Part P below: the harm comes from components the labels call free. Part P above half and Part Z below: the harm comes from needed components turned down to their labels, so the label values are too low. Both parts determined and on the same side: no single attribution; the post says which case occurred, since both below half means the harm needs both kinds of position together and both above half means either kind alone suffices. Either part undetermined: the split is undetermined and the post keeps "a setting the labels permit".

## 3. The On-or-Off Histogram (description)

> **Settings.** All 160 searches' saved settings at 20, 40, 80, 160, and 320 steps, and the one-step-earlier copies at 160 and 320, attack and held-out sides reported separately. No GPU.
>
> **Bins.** Exactly 0; exactly 1; and the open interval in ten equal bins. Reported for three groups of entries: never-needed components, ever-needed components, and the 24 delta entries. An entry is **untouched** at a saved step when its saved absolute gradient sum at that step is exactly zero (it then equals its start, which is checked); an interior entry that is not untouched is **turned**. Both counts are printed.

## 4. Exploited Components (signed line: 90 percent) and the Pin Trigger

> **Settings.** All 80 attack searches at 80 and 320 steps, with 40 steps as description. Each setting is scored on the held-out batch with its attack batch's index ($A_i$ on $H_i$).
>
> **Ranking.** Entry $c$ is ranked, largest first, by $a_c = G_c\,(s_c - s^0_c)$, where $G_c$ is the saved signed cumulative gradient at that step, $s_c$ the saved value, and $s^0_c$ the start; ties go to the lower entry index, and the delta entries rank with the rest. This is the first-order estimate of what moving entry $c$ from its start to its final value adds, which is the intervention scored below. As description at 320 steps, the curve is also printed with entries ranked by $|G_c|$, the plan's literal "gradient accumulated over the search".
>
> **The intervention.** The top $k$ entries keep their final values and every other entry is set to the start's value. The kept share at $k$ is pooled over the 80 settings of a length: the sum over settings of (harm of the top-$k$ setting minus the start's harm) divided by the sum of (saved carried harm minus the start's harm). The grid is $k \in \{10, 20, 30, 50, 100, 200, 300, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000, 5000, 10000, 20000, 38936\}$. $k = 0$ must reproduce the saved start harm and $k = 38{,}936$ the saved carried harm, both bit for bit.
>
> **The count.** The **pooled count** is the smallest grid $k$ whose pooled kept share is at least 0.90, stated as "at most $k$": the count is an upper bound, since a ranking can miss entries whose effect the gradient does not show. Beside it: the smallest $k$ keeping 0.50; the pooled count from each start separately; the median, quartiles, and range of the per-setting counts; and the median among settings whose own harm had passed 5 nats by that step. Every count is set beside the 9,972 alive components and is never compared with a per-position count. No interval.
>
> **The random set.** The same size as the top set, matched to it cell by cell in (matrix, stratum): in each cell as many entries as the top set has there, drawn uniformly without replacement, the delta entries one extra stratum. Random entries keep their final values and the rest sit at the start. One draw per $k$, keyed by (search, length, $k$, draw); three draws at $k = 3{,}000$ at 320 steps. As description, a uniform draw over all entries at $k = 3{,}000$.
>
> **The pin trigger.** Read once, at 320 steps, on pooled shares under the ranking $a_c$ above: it fires if the top 3,000 keep at least 0.90 and the matched random 3,000 (mean of three draws) keep under 0.50. The same two numbers at 80 steps, and the 3,000 figure of the $|G_c|$-ranked curve, are printed and do not fire it.

## 5. Whether the Exploited Components Are Switched Off or Left On (description)

> At 80 and 320 steps, for the top set at the pooled count and at 3,000: the share of its entries at exactly 0, in (0, 0.5], in (0.5, 1), and at exactly 1, split by alive component (with usage band), never-needed component, and delta entry, beside the same shares over all entries. Then two parts are scored, read as shares of the top set's kept added harm and allowed not to add up: the **off part**, in which only the top entries at exactly 0 keep their final values and everything else sits at the start; and the **on part**, in which only the top entries at exactly 1 keep theirs. As description, the top set is also scored on a background of 1 (every other component fully on), with the all-ones harm beside it: "set these $k$ components as the adversary did, leave the model otherwise intact, and the damage is $X$ nats".

## 6. How the Harm Is Spread Over Texts (description; no GPU)

> For every carried cell at each of the five search lengths and three harm levels, a text's added harm is its harm under the setting minus its harm under the same start on the same text. Four statistics over the batch's 128 texts: the share of texts whose harm exceeds the 0.75-nat benchmark; the harm of the 10th-percentile text (the 13th lowest); the share of the cell's added harm carried by its 13 most-harmed texts, with negative added harms counted as zero (0.10 is an even spread); and the median text's added harm divided by the mean, with negative values counted as zero in both. The share of texts with negative added harm is printed beside the last two. Each statistic is summarized over cells by its median with the 10th and 90th percentiles, with no interval, and the same four are printed for the held-out batches' own searches and for the shuffled copies as reference rows. For whether the same texts are hit: the mean over cells of the Spearman correlation, over the 128 texts, between a carried setting's added harm and, in turn, the held-out batch's own search (same start, read at the same length or, in the harm-level rows, at the same harm level), a carried setting from another attack batch (same start), the carried setting from the same attack batch and another start, and the start's own per-text harm. One limit is stated with it: a text's harm is a mean over 512 positions, so "most texts" does not imply "most positions".

## 7. Agreement Between Settings (description; no GPU)

> Two settings agree on an entry when both values are below one half or both are at or above one half. For each pair, over the 38,912 rank-one entries, with alive and never-needed entries also reported apart and the delta entries apart: the share that agree and a chance level. For pairs from different starts, chance is the agreement expected if the two settings were independent within each stratum: $\sum_g (n_g/N)\,[p_{a,g}p_{b,g} + (1-p_{a,g})(1-p_{b,g})]$, with $p_{a,g}$ setting $a$'s share of entries in stratum $g$ below one half. For pairs from the same start, which begin from identical values, chance is the agreement expected if, within each stratum, whether each setting has crossed one half from that start were independent: the same formula with the crossing shares $q$ in place of $p$. Agreement beyond chance is $(\bar A - \bar C)/(1 - \bar C)$ from the family means. For same-start pairs it is also printed with the entries untouched in both settings (Section 3) left out, since those agree by construction. Also reported: the overlap of the two settings' top 3,000 entries under Section 4's ranking, against the overlap expected if each top set were drawn at random within strata in its own numbers, $\sum_g k_{a,g}k_{b,g}/n_g$, and among overlapping entries the share on the same side of one half. The families are: same start and different batches; different starts and the same batch; different starts and different batches. The pairs come from every search from starts 9 to 13 (the run's 160 and the starts test's 40) and from the pilot's searches from starts 1 to 8 on the calibration batches if their settings are saved at the same steps. Every search length and harm level is computed; 320 steps is quoted.

## 8. Three Cheap Controls and the Rounding Check (description)

> **Cell set S4.** Every carried setting the run saved (80 attack searches at the five lengths, 400 settings) scored on four held-out batches fixed by stride: H1, H5, H9, and H13. The saved per-text harms of the same settings and of the five starts on those batches are the reference.
>
> **(a) The delta components' share.** Each setting in S4 is re-scored with its 24 delta entries set to 1 and every other entry unchanged, and each start the same way on the four batches. Per length, the share of carried added harm remaining is pooled: the sum over cells of (harm of the reset setting minus harm of the reset start) over the sum of (saved harm of the setting minus saved harm of the start), with the same share from one start at a time as its spread. Beside it, from the saved settings alone, the count of delta entries at exactly 0, exactly 1, and between.
>
> **(b1) Every mask at its label.** The setting with every entry at 0, delta entries included, scored once on each of the 16 held-out and 16 attack batches with per-text harms saved; the mean over batches, printed beside the start's 0.22 and the first post's 0.34 on its own texts.
>
> **(b2) The first post's aggregated explanations: its construction, with donors drawn from its recipient texts.** (The first post drew donors from a separate 1,024-text set; both sets are excluded from our pools, so the held-out batches share nothing with them.) Eight seeded draws of 64 positions, uniform over the 524,288 positions of the first post's 1,024 texts, from the float32 label cache the strata were built from; a component enters the union if its label at any drawn position is strictly above 0.1; the setting is 1 on the union, 0 elsewhere, delta entries at 0; each draw scored on each of the 16 held-out batches. Also eight draws of 16 positions. Per rung: the mean harm, the added harm above (b1), the draw-to-draw range, and the union's size, beside the first post's 0.80, 0.46, and about 4,544 components.
>
> **(c) Two coarser shuffles.** For each setting in S4, one shuffled copy of each kind, delta entries in place, with a fresh seed label ("s6-coarse-shuffle", kind, batch, start, length): within alive and never-needed only (the ten bands merged), and over the whole matrix. Per kind and length: the copies' mean harm beside the start's 0.22 and the run's band-matched 0.30 to 0.32; the share of alive entries moved by more than 0.5; and, for the whole-matrix kind, the share of the setting's entries at 0 that land on never-needed components.
>
> **(d) Rounding.** Each setting in S4 is re-scored with every entry, delta entries included, rounded to 0 below 0.5 and to 1 otherwise, and each start the same way. Per length, the share of carried added harm remaining is pooled: the sum over cells of (harm of the rounded setting minus saved harm of the start) over the sum of (saved harm of the setting minus saved harm of the start); the rounded start's harm is printed beside it. A second variant rounds only the entries that moved from their start by at least 0.05 and leaves the rest at the start, to separate the search's choice from the entries left at or near their start.

## 9. The Pin Test (runs only if Section 4's trigger fires)

> For attack batches A1 to A4 from each of the five starts, 20 searches: a fresh 320-step search from the same start with that (batch, start) search's own top 3,000 entries at 320 steps held at 1. Reported: each search's harm record beside the original's; the 320-step harm as a share of the original's; and each new setting carried to S4's four held-out batches, with the carry-over read as the retention of Appendix A (description, no line). The pin constrains only entries the adversary moved down; entries it already held at 1 are unchanged by pinning, so the test tests the off part.

## 11. Hypotheses on File Before the Data

Four independent sets of hypotheses were filed (research log); they converge on three, and one set adds a fourth. They are recorded here with the readings that would tell them apart, so that the results are read against them rather than fitted to them. They are not exclusive; the shares would say how much of each.

| Hypothesis | What it says | Favoured by | Disfavoured by | Cheap test proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Broken cancellation among free components** | Many components the labels call free each push the output a little with a sign that is consistent across texts; the pushes cancel under random masks and under all-at-label, and a shared setting that switches off one side and keeps the other on breaks the cancellation the same way on every text (the paper's own toy case at line 2017, made systematic) | Part Z above half; top entries at both 0 and 1; a count in the thousands; most texts hit; rounding keeps the harm | Part P above half; a count in the tens or hundreds; top entries nearly all at 0 | The complement: re-score 320-step settings with every entry replaced by 1 minus itself; a cancellation-breaking pattern's complement also breaks it and should do large harm, while B predicts the complement does modest harm, since it switches the needed components back on |
| **B. Needed labels set too low** | The penalty on every label above zero, and a random-mask loss that rarely samples the floor, leave needed labels at the lowest value a weak per-position search tolerates; turning many needed components down to their labels at once was never tested | Part P above half; top entries at 0 among alive, heavily used components; the alive-and-never-needed shuffle doing more harm than the band-matched one | Part Z above half; top entries mostly never-needed | Raise the floor: re-score with every positive label below 0.3 lifted to 0.3; or raise the setting to at least 0.1 where labels are positive |
| **C. Training's adversary was too local and too weak to reach the flaw** | One source per position, three small steps per batch, one text's gradient at a time, ending training at 0.57 nats, before breakout; the shared search pools 65,536 positions per step. An account of the search, which combines with A or B | A large count; entries at the bounds; most texts; low agreement across starts; the pin test's fresh search finding a second set | A few heavy components, which a per-position adversary would have seen | Run training's own adversary against the frozen released decomposition and record whether it ever breaks out; or run the shared search on one document and carry it, to see whether a per-document adversary would have seen the flaw |
| **D. Cross-matrix combinations were never defended** | If training masked a random subset of the 24 matrices per step (the paper's line 2169), combinations needing several matrices at once were rarely presented | Exploited components spread over many matrices (weak) | Exploited components in one or two matrices | Apply the setting to one matrix at a time, the rest at the start, 24 passes per cell on a few cells |

Two facts to settle before the plain page leans on them: whether the released run's training adversary routed all 24 matrices or a random subset (the paper's line 2169 says a subset; `adversary.py`'s docstring and, unverified, the authors' `persistent_pgd.py` suggest all); and whether any completed run trained with the per-document adversary exists: the one reference run whose configuration was read (`p-8383f5e5`) trained with per-position settings, and the per-document form appears only in a newer configuration file (research log, Section 7), so the post may not imply a decomposition trained that way exists unless one is found.

## Addendum, 7 October 2026, after the histogram and before any GPU result

Three small changes, all description, prompted by the histogram. Nothing else changes.

1. **Section 5, off and on parts.** The histogram shows that from 80 steps about one entry in ten sits one step from a bound (in [0.1, 0.2) or [0.8, 0.9)), alternating with it. So at 320 steps only, beside the exact-bound parts, compute two variants at every grid $k$: an off part counting entries at most 0.1 and an on part counting entries at least 0.9, read the same way. About 150 passes per held-out container.
2. **Section 8(d), the second rounding variant.** Nothing in the saved settings is untouched, so the moved-only variant is nearly plain rounding. Replace it with a snap variant: entries at most 0.2 go to 0, entries at least 0.8 go to 1, and every other entry keeps its value. Same cell set and cost as before.
3. **Section 7, agreement (plan 3 of the research log, CPU).** Beside the committed same-start chance level, print a chance level computed within deciles of the shared start value, since at 20 and 40 steps entries not yet at a bound still lean toward their start.
